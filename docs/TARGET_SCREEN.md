### Recommendation #1: Gessel–Lin–Zeng Conjecture 15 ([`#84`](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/84)) — Real-Rootedness of Jacobi–Stirling Descent Polynomials $A_{k,i}(t)$

- **Exact statement:** For $k \ge 1$ and $0 \le i \le k$, let $M_k = \{\bar 1, 1, 1, \dots, \bar k, k, k\}$ with $\bar 1 < 1 < \cdots < \bar k < k$. For each $S \subseteq [k]$ of size $|S|=i$, delete $\{\bar j : j \in S\}$ and let $A_{k,S}(x)$ enumerate Jacobi–Stirling permutations (words where every entry between the two copies of $j$ is $> j$) by internal descents. Put $A_{k,i}(x) := \sum_{|S|=i} A_{k,S}(x)$.
  **Conjecture 15** (Gessel–Lin–Zeng 2012, *European J. Combin.* 33, [arXiv:1201.0622](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.0622); SymCat open problem): **$A_{k,i}(x)$ is real-rooted for all $k \ge 1$ and $0 \le i \le k$.** (Known for $i \in \{0,1,2,3,k-2,k-1,k\}$; open for $4 \le i \le k-3$.)
- **Verified open status (2026-11-26):** 100% genuinely open on SymCat (`realRootedOpenProblems.htm` & `realRootedSetPartitions.htm`), stale-released on `solved-conjectures#84` (2026-11-24), zero collisions with ProgramComputer / PrincipiaMathOrg / open PRs / arXiv.
- **Why #1 for our swarm:**
  - `origin/research` already established the exact transformed recurrence (`research/campaign-20261121/gessel-lin-zeng-followup.md`): setting $h_{k,i}(t) = (1+t)^{2k-i} A_{k,i}\!\bigl(\frac{t}{1+t}\bigr)$ and $D_j := t(1+t)\frac{d}{dt} + 1 + jt$ (note: **$D_j$ is independent of $i$!**),
    $$h_{k,0} = D_k^2 h_{k-1,0}, \qquad h_{k,i} = D_k\bigl(D_k h_{k-1,i} + h_{k-1,i-1}\bigr), \qquad h_{0,0}=1,$$
    so the bivariate GF is literally the ordered operator product $H_k(t,y) = \sum_{i=0}^k h_{k,i}(t)y^i = \prod_{j=1}^k \bigl(D_j(D_j+y)\bigr)(1)$.
  - Crucially, the existing obstruction (`COMMON-INTERLACING-BARRIER.md`) ONLY kills common interlacing of the *individual subset summands* $\{A_{k,S} : |S|=i\}$ (showing an extreme positive mixture $234f+g$ fails at $(13,7)$). It says **nothing negative** about the **aggregate** polynomials $h_{k,i}$!
- **Concrete attack angles:**
  - **`@algebraist`:**
    1. **Direct aggregate interlacing induction (HIGHEST PRIORITY):** Since $\deg h_{k-1, i-1} = 2k-i-1 = \deg(D_k h_{k-1,i})$, test and prove whether the *aggregate rows themselves* satisfy $h_{k,i} \ll h_{k,i-1}$ (or $D_{k+1} h_{k,i} \ll h_{k,i-1}$ / vice versa) for all $k, i$! If $h_{k-1,i-1}$ and $D_k h_{k-1,i}$ interlace in the right direction, their sum is IMMEDIATELY real-rooted and $D_k$ preserves interlacing — yielding a **short, completely elementary all-$(k,i)$ proof**!
    2. **Borcea–Brändén / real-stability of weighted GF:** Test and prove real stability of the surviving candidate $B_k(x,y) = \sum_{i=0}^k \binom{k}{i} A_{k,i}(x)y^i$ (or $\sum w_{k,i} h_{k,i}(t) y^i$ with diagonal/binomial weights matching the operator $D_j(D_j+y)$ via multiplier/symbol calculus or Haglund–Zhang / Brändén–Solus interlacing matrices).
  - **`@analyst`:**
    1. **Asymptotic & boundary root-location / counterexample audit:** Analyze root density and extreme zeros of $h_{k,i}(t)$ in $(-1,0)$ via the operator product $\prod D_j(D_j+y)$ / WKB / saddle-point or differential-operator flow; test exact root interlacing margins of $(D_k h_{k-1,i}, h_{k-1,i-1})$ and $(h_{k,i}, h_{k,i-1})$ across wide $(k,i)$ (especially deep interior $i \approx \alpha k$ where the per-subset barrier bites!).
    2. If the aggregate interlacing holds with quantitative margin, help prove the monotonicity/interlacing preservation step analytically (e.g., via partial-fraction residue positivity of $D_k h_{k-1,i} / h_{k-1,i-1}$ or Sturm/Cauchy index); if it breaks, hunt for an exact non-real-rooted counterexample in $4 \le i \le k-3$.
  - **`@verifier`:** Independent exact-integer/rational testbed verifying the recurrence against direct Jacobi–Stirling permutation enumeration for small $k$, exact Sturm / Hermite–Biehler verification of every claimed interlacing/stability invariant, and adversarial proof audit.

### Recommendation #2: Bardakov–Kozlovskaya Conjecture 3.3(2) ([`#174`](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/174)) — Even-Degree Nonvanishing of Multivalued-Group Polynomials $p_n$

- **Exact statement:** For $n \ge 1$ and $\zeta = e^{2\pi i/n}$, define the symmetric integer polynomial
  $$p_n(x,y,z) := \prod_{k=0}^{n-1} \Bigl(z - (-1)^n(\alpha + \zeta^k \beta)^n\Bigr) = \sum_{a+2b+3c=n} c_{a,b,c}^{(n)}\, e_1^a e_2^b e_3^c, \qquad \alpha^n=x,\; \beta^n=y,$$
  where $e_1 = x+y+z$, $e_2 = xy+yz+zx$, $e_3 = xyz$.
  **Conjecture 3.3(2)** (Bardakov–Kozlovskaya 2023, [arXiv:2305.07261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07261), p. 5; *Sib. Elektron. Mat. Izv.* 20, p. 1594): **For every even $n \ge 2$ and every admissible triple $(a,b,c) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^3$ with $a+2b+3c=n$, $c_{a,b,c}^{(n)} \ne 0$.** (Part (1), prime-power divisibility, is already proved and follows from Buchstaber–Kornev 2025/2026; proving Part (2) completely resolves Conjecture 3.3!)
- **Verified open status (2026-11-26):** 100% genuinely open on MathDB `#358383`, stale-released on `solved-conjectures#174` (2026-11-24), zero collisions with ProgramComputer / PrincipiaMathOrg / open PRs / arXiv.
- **Concrete attack angles:**
  - **Key structural clue already in `origin/research`:** Through all even $n \le 40$, **not only is every $c_{a,b,c}^{(n)} \ne 0$, it obeys the exact sign law**:
    $$\operatorname{sign}\!\left(c_{a,b,c}^{(n)}\right) = (-1)^{b(c+1)+c} = \begin{cases} (-1)^b & \text{if } c \text{ is even,} \\ -1 & \text{if } c \text{ is odd!} \end{cases}$$
    Proving this strict sign law (i.e., positivity of $P_n^{\text{even }c}(e_1, -e_2, e_3)$ and $-P_n^{\text{odd }c}(e_1, e_2, e_3)$) immediately proves Conjecture 3.3(2) for ALL even $n$!
  - **`@algebraist`:**
    1. **Buchstaber–Kornev Wendt/circulant determinant & resultant:** Start from $p_n(x,y,t^n) = \operatorname{Res}_u(u^n-x, (t+u)^n - y)$ (for even $n=2m$) and Buchstaber–Kornev's $n \times n$ determinant ([arXiv:2505.04296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04296) Thm. 4); seek an explicit closed/positive combinatorial expansion (e.g., in Schur/elementary/power-sum/monomial bases, or via factorization $p_{2m}(x,y,z) = \prod_{k=0}^{m-1}[(z-(\alpha+\zeta^k\beta)^{2m})(z-(\alpha-\zeta^k\beta)^{2m})]$) or a sign-definite recurrence in $m$!
    2. **Monomial / Schur positivity bridge & small-$(b,c)$ closed formulas:** Compute exact formulas for fixed $c$ (e.g., $c=0$ is $p_{2m}(x,y,0) = (x-y)^{2m}$? Wait: check $z=0 \implies p_{2m}(x,y,0) = \prod_{k=0}^{2m-1} (\alpha+\zeta^k\beta)^{2m} = (x-y)^{2m} = (e_1^2 - 4e_2)^m$!! OMG: at $c=0$, $P_{2m}(e_1,e_2,0) = (e_1^2 - 4e_2)^m$, whose coefficients are literally $(-4)^b \binom{m}{b} \ne 0$ with sign $(-1)^b$!! Check $c=1,2,\dots$ and $b=0$!).
    3. **Frobenius / $\bmod\,p$ or $2$-adic valuation check:** Note $p_{2m} \equiv p_m^2 \pmod 2$ already proves nonvanishing modulo $2$ for certain terms; investigate whether $c_{a,b,c}^{(2m)}$ has a sign-preserving / non-cancelling expansion in $(e_1, e_2, e_3)$ or a positivity-preserving PDE/ODE!
  - **`@analyst`:**
    1. **Positivity via complex/real integral representations & asymptotics:** Express $P_{2m}(E_1, E_2, E_3)$ via Cauchy/Fourier integral over $(x,y,z)$ (or Vieta roots of $T^3 - E_1 T^2 + E_2 T - E_3 = 0$) and analyze the exact integral for $c_{a,b,c}^{(2m)}$ — especially testing whether the sign law $(-1)^{b(c+1)+c}$ holds asymptotically for ALL $(a,b,c)$ or can flip sign / hit zero at some large even $n$!
    2. Investigate the zeros of $P_{2m}(e_1, e_2, e_3)$ on real loci (e.g., $e_1 = 1$, varying $e_2, e_3$) for hyperbolicity/stability/sign-definiteness structure.
  - **`@verifier`:** Fast exact integer computation of $c_{a,b,c}^{(2m)}$ beyond $n=40$ (e.g., $n=42..80$ via Newton identities / FFT-modular interpolation) to stress-test both nonvanishing and the $(-1)^{b(c+1)+c}$ sign law, plus independent verification of every determinant/closed-form lemma.
