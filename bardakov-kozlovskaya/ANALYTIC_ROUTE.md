# Analytic Route to Bardakov–Kozlovskaya Conjecture 3.3(2): Complete Regime 2 Proof ($\kappa < 2.5$)

**Target Conjecture ([arXiv:2305.07261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07261), Conjecture 3.3(2)):**
For every even integer $n = 2m \ge 2$ and every $(a,b,c) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^3$ with $a + 2b + 3c = 2m$, the coefficient $c_{a,b,c}^{(2m)}$ of $e_1^a e_2^b e_3^c$ in $p_{2m}(x,y,z) = \prod_{k=0}^{2m-1}(z - (\alpha + \zeta^k \beta)^{2m})$ is nonzero and satisfies $\operatorname{sign}(c_{a,b,c}^{(2m)}) = (-1)^{b(c+1)+c}$.

By `ALGEBRA_ROUTE.md` (Theorems 3.1–3.2 and Section 4, independently certified by `@verifier` in `#39`, `#42`, `#45`, `#57`, `#58`–`#61`), the conjecture is reduced to proving the strict sign inequality
$$(-1)^j Q_c^{(2m)}(1, u_j) > 0$$
for all strictly interior sample points $(n, c, j)$ in **Regime 2**:
$$n = 2m \ge 132, \qquad 2 \le c \le \Bigl\lfloor\frac{n-3}{3}\Bigr\rfloor, \qquad j \in \mathcal{J}_{n,c} := \bigl\{c+1,\dots, m - \lceil c/2 \rceil - \mathbf{1}_{c\text{ even}}\bigr\}, \qquad \kappa(n,c,j) < 2.5,$$
where $\theta_c := \frac{c\pi}{n}$, $\psi_j := \frac{j\pi}{n}$, $\alpha_1 := \frac{(j+c)\pi}{n}$, $r_1 := \sin\alpha_1$, $r_2 := \sin\psi_j$, $r_3 := \sin\theta_c$, $s_j := r_2/r_1 \in (1/2, 1)$, $R_y := s_j^n$, $R_z := (r_3/r_1)^n$, and $\kappa := 2\pi \frac{r_1 r_2}{r_3}$.

## 1. Lemma R2.1: Universal Regime 2 Cubic-Root Perturbation ($\kappa < 2.5, n \ge 132$)

### Statement
Let $(n, c, j)$ satisfy $n = 2m \ge 132$, $2 \le c \le \lfloor(n-3)/3\rfloor$, $j \in \mathcal{J}_{n,c}$, and $\kappa < \kappa_0 := 2.5$. Then:
1. **Angle bounds:** Since $j \ge c + 1$ implies $\psi_j > \theta_c$ and $\alpha_1 > 2\theta_c$, we have $\sin\alpha_1 = \frac{\kappa\sin\theta_c}{2\pi\sin\psi_j} < \frac{\kappa}{2\pi}$, hence
   $$\alpha_1 < \alpha_{\max} := 0.409284, \qquad \theta_c < \frac{\alpha_{\max}}{2}, \qquad S_\alpha := \frac{\sin\alpha_{\max}}{\alpha_{\max}} \ge 0.972314, \qquad \cos(2\alpha_{\max}) \ge 0.683268.$$
2. **Universal root-separation bounds:**
   $$R_z \le \Bigl(\frac{1}{2\cos(\alpha_{\max}/2)}\Bigr)^n \le 2.971\times 10^{-39}, \qquad R_y \le e^{-2\pi\cot\alpha_{\max}} \le 5.121\times 10^{-7},$$
   $$\frac{R_z}{R_y} \le e^{-\pi\cot(\alpha_{\max}/2)} \le 2.668\times 10^{-7}, \qquad \frac{R_z}{R_y} \le \exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{C_{\exp}}{\kappa}\Bigr) \quad (C_{\exp} \ge 37.8481),$$
   $$\frac{1}{\theta_c}\frac{R_z}{R_y} \le 2.025\times 10^{-4} \qquad \text{(uniformly for all } n \ge 132\text{)}.$$
3. **Rouché & holomorphic perturbation on $|w| = 1$ (ALL $\kappa < 2.5$):**
   Lemma 4.2 of `ALGEBRA_ROUTE.md` holds on $|w| \le 1$ with $\rho_x = 10^{-40}$ and $u_y = 1.001\,R_z/R_y$, yielding
   $$t_1 \le 4.017\times 10^{-43}, \qquad t_2 \le 1.000\times 10^{-6}, \qquad \eta_{\text{pert}} \le 2.998\times 10^{-7}, \qquad R_{\text{bdy}} \le 1.733\times 10^{-6}.$$
   Moreover, for all $n \ge N_0 = 132$ with $q := (R_z/R_y)^{(N_0-1)/N_0} \le \exp(-C_{\text{exp,pert}}/\kappa)$ ($C_{\text{exp,pert}} := \frac{131}{132} C_{\exp} \ge 37.5614$), the $n$-uniform boundary perturbation satisfies $R_{\text{bdy}} \le C_{\text{bdy}}\, q$ with $C_{\text{bdy}} \le 6.0061$, and every small root $|\eta_k| < \theta_c$ satisfies $|1 - s_j e^{i\eta_k}| \ge 1 - s_j > (r_3/r_1)\cos\alpha_{\max}$, giving $|a_k| \le \frac{r_2}{r_3\cos\alpha_{\max}}$ and uniform off-boundary perturbation coefficient $\eta_{3,\text{coeff}} := \frac{1.005}{\cos\alpha_{\max}} + 1.005 \le 2.0996$.
4. **Rescaled contour $|w| = \lambda \le 3$ inside $c\kappa^2 < C_1 := 42$:**
   Since $\kappa = 2\pi\frac{\sin\psi_j\sin\alpha_1}{\sin\theta_c} \ge 2\pi S_\alpha^2 \frac{j(j+c)}{cn} \ge 2\pi S_\alpha^2 \frac{j^2}{cn}$ and $c\kappa^2 < C_1$, writing $\alpha_1 = \frac{(j+c)\pi}{n}$ gives $\frac{\alpha_1}{2} \le K_{jc} n^{-1/3}$ where $K_{jc} := \bigl(\frac{C_1}{2\pi^4 S_\alpha^4}\bigr)^{1/3}$, and hence the stretched-exponential decay
   $$\frac{R_z}{R_y} \le \exp\bigl(-c_{\text{se}}\, n^{1/3}\bigr) \qquad \bigl(c_{\text{se}} := \tfrac{2(\alpha_{\max}/2)\cot(\alpha_{\max}/2)}{K_{jc}} \ge 3.16794\bigr),$$
   yielding $n(R_z/R_y) \le 1.304\times 10^{-5}$ uniformly for all $n \ge 132$.
5. **Pigeonhole-Annulus Lemma (Piece G3 contour reduction for ALL $\lambda_* \in [0.05, 3.00]$):**
   Inside Piece G3 ($r_0\kappa \le 0.45, c\kappa^2 < 42 \implies \alpha_1 \le 0.07169, n \ge N_{\text{G3}} = 1007, c_{\text{se,G3}} \ge 3.330$), combining the constant bound $R_z/R_y \le 9.01\times 10^{-39}$ (for $n \le N_{\text{sp}} = 50000$) with the stretched-exponential bound $e^{-c_{\text{se,G3}} n^{1/3}}$ (strictly decreasing in $n \ge 50000$ since $(12/c_{\text{se,eff}})^3 < 50 < 50000$) certifies $M_4 := \sup_{n \ge 1007} n^4(R_z/R_y)^{1-1/n} \le 6.14\times 10^{-20}$ and $M_3 := \sup_{n \ge 1007} n^3(R_z/R_y)^{1-1/n} \le 1.23\times 10^{-24}$. Partitioning $[\lambda_*(1-\varepsilon_{\text{ph}}), \lambda_*(1+\varepsilon_{\text{ph}})]$ ($\varepsilon_{\text{ph}} := 10^{-5}/n$) into $n$ equal subintervals and selecting the midpoint $\tilde\lambda$ of a slice free of all $\le n - 1$ forbidden radii $\{|a_k|^{-1}, 1\}$ ensures $|1 - (-1)^j w^{-1}| \ge \frac{\varepsilon_{\text{ph}}}{(1+\varepsilon_{\text{ph}})n}$ and $\frac{|a_k w|}{|1 - a_k w|} \le \frac{2 n^2}{0.49\times 10^{-5}}$ on $|w| = \tilde\lambda$, certifying $|\log(F_{\text{full}}/F_{\text{pure}})| \le E_{\text{cubic,G3}} \le 2.80\times 10^{-6}$. Since $P(w) := w^{c+1} F_{\text{pure}}(w)$ is a polynomial of degree $n$, the Poisson-kernel $L^1$ inequality applied to $P(w)$ and $z^n P(1/z)$ gives $M_1(F_{\text{pure}}; \tilde\lambda) \le (1-\varepsilon_{\text{ph}})^{-n} M_1(F_{\text{pure}}; \lambda_*) \le 1.000010\, M_1(F_{\text{pure}}; \lambda_*)$, while Cauchy's theorem gives $\int F_{\text{pure}}(\tilde\lambda e^{i\phi})\,d\phi = \int F_{\text{pure}}(\lambda_* e^{i\phi})\,d\phi$. Hence
   $$\frac{\operatorname{Re}\int_{-\pi}^\pi F_{\text{full}}(\tilde\lambda e^{i\phi})\,d\phi}{\int_{-\pi}^\pi |F_{\text{pure}}(\lambda_* e^{i\phi})|\,d\phi} \;\ge\; \frac{\operatorname{Re}\int_{-\pi}^\pi F_{\text{pure}}(\lambda_* e^{i\phi})\,d\phi}{\int_{-\pi}^\pi |F_{\text{pure}}(\lambda_* e^{i\phi})|\,d\phi} \;-\; 2.80\times 10^{-6}.$$

*Proof.* Items (1)–(3) follow directly from convexity of $\cot x$ (Lemma 4.1 of `ALGEBRA_ROUTE.md`) and the decreasing monotonicity of $x\cot x$ on $(0, \pi/2)$: $\pi\cot(\alpha_1/2) = \frac{2\pi}{\alpha_1}\cdot(\frac{\alpha_1}{2}\cot\frac{\alpha_1}{2}) \ge \frac{4\pi^2 S_\alpha}{\kappa}(\frac{\alpha_{\max}}{2}\cot\frac{\alpha_{\max}}{2}) = C_{\exp}/\kappa$. For $\frac{1}{\theta_c}\frac{R_z}{R_y}$, splitting $\psi_j \le 2\theta_c$ (where $\alpha_1/2 \le \frac{3}{2}\theta_c$ gives $\frac{1}{\theta_c}e^{-c_\theta/\theta_c}$, decreasing for $\theta_c \le \theta_{c,\max}$) versus $\psi_j > 2\theta_c$ (where $r_3/r_2 < \frac{1}{2\cos\theta_c}$ gives $\frac{n}{2\pi} r_{\max}^n$, decreasing for $n \ge 132$) yields the uniform bound. For (4), $c\kappa^2 \ge \frac{4\pi^4 S_\alpha^4}{n^2}\frac{j^2(j+c)^2}{c} \ge \frac{2\pi^4 S_\alpha^4}{n^2}(j+c)^3$ (since $j > c \implies j^2/c > (j+c)/2$? More directly, since $j \ge c+1 > c$, $j^2(j+c)^2/c = (j/c)\cdot j(j+c)^2 > \frac{1}{2}(j+c)^3$ because $2 j^2 > c(j+c) \iff (2j+c)(j-c) > 0$!), so $\frac{j+c}{n} < \bigl(\frac{C_1}{2\pi^4 S_\alpha^4}\bigr)^{1/3} n^{-1/3}$. Certified in 50-digit `mpmath.iv` by [`certify_R2_1_and_2A_iv.py`](analyst/certify_R2_1_and_2A_iv.py). $\square$

## 2. Lemma R2.2: Sub-regime 2A ($c\kappa^2 \ge 42, \kappa < 2.5, n \ge 132$)

### Statement
For every strictly interior $(n, c, j)$ with $n \ge 132$, $\kappa < 2.5$, and $c\kappa^2 \ge C_1 := 42$, the Theorem 3.2 integral on $|w| = 1$ satisfies
$$\frac{\operatorname{Re}\int_{-\pi}^\pi (1 + \Delta_{\text{mult}}(e^{i\phi}))\, B(e^{i\phi})\,d\phi}{\int_{-\pi}^\pi (2 - 2\cos(\phi + j\pi))\, |F_3^{\text{pure}}(e^{i\phi})|\,d\phi} \;\ge\; +0.220613 \;>\; 0,$$
and therefore $(-1)^j Q_c^{(2m)}(1, u_j) > 0$.

### Proof
On $|w| = 1$, write $Z = (-1)^j w^{-1} = e^{-i\psi}$ ($\psi = \phi + j\pi$) so $B^{\text{pure}}(e^{i\phi}) = 2 - 2\cos\psi = 4\sin^2(\psi/2) \ge 0$, and decompose $F_3^{\text{pure}}(e^{i\phi}) = \prod_{\text{small}}(1 - u_k e^{-i\psi}) \prod_{\text{large}}(1 - v_k e^{i\psi})$ with $|u_k| < 1$, $|v_k| < 1$.

1. **Exact small-root / large-root pairing phase bound $B_{2A} < \pi/2$:**
   Pair each small root $\eta_k = \pm(\theta_c - \frac{2m\pi}{n})$ ($1 \le m \le \lfloor c/2 \rfloor$) with the opposite large root $\eta_{k'} = \pm(\theta_c + \frac{2m\pi}{n})$: their product satisfies the exact trigonometric identity
   $$\frac{v_m}{u_m} = \Bigl(\frac{1 - s_j e^{i(\theta_c - 2m\pi/n)}}{1 - s_j e^{i(\theta_c + 2m\pi/n)}}\Bigr)^n = (1 + \zeta_m)^{-n}$$
   (verified to $< 10^{-69}$ in 80-digit arithmetic), giving $|\arg(1 - u_m e^{-i\psi})(1 - v_m e^{i\psi})| \le |\arg(1 - u_m e^{-i\psi}) + \arg(1 - v_m e^{i\psi})|$ whose leading terms cancel up to $O(m^2/(c\kappa))$! Splitting the small-root index at $m \le f c$ ($f = 0.26$) versus $m \in (fc, \lfloor c/2 \rfloor]$ (plus any unpaired middle root $m = c/2$ and unpaired large roots $m > c/2$), with $L := c\kappa \ge C_1/\kappa_0 = 16.8$, $\gamma_{\text{ext}} := \operatorname{sinc}(\alpha_{\max}/4)$, $\gamma_z := (\alpha_{\max}/2)/\sin(\alpha_{\max}/2)$, and $M_g := \max_{t>0} \frac{t^2}{e^t - 1}$ (uniquely attained at $t_0 \in [1.59362426, 1.59362427]$ where $(2-t_0)e^{t_0} = 2$), yields
   $$|\arg F_3^{\text{pure}}(e^{i\phi})| \;\le\; B_{2A} := S_1 + S_2 + S_{\text{mid}} + S_{\text{int}} \;\le\; 1.332945 \;<\; \frac{\pi}{2},$$
   hence $\cos(B_{2A}) \ge 0.235615 > 0$ identically on the entire unit circle $|w| = 1$.

2. **Exact perturbation audit ($\Delta_{123}$ and $\Delta_B$):**
   - For $\Delta_{123} := F_1 F_2 (F_3/F_3^{\text{pure}}) - 1$: summing $\frac{|u_k|}{1-|u_k|}$ and $\frac{|v_k|}{1-|v_k|}$ (using $(1+x)^{-M} \le (1+Mx)^{-1}$) and multiplying by $\eta_{3,\text{coeff}}\, e^{-C_{\text{exp,pert}}/\kappa}$ (monotone increasing on $\kappa \in (0, 2.5]$ since $C_{\text{exp,pert}}/2 > 18.7 \gg 2.5$) yields $|\Delta_{123}| \le 8.246\times 10^{-6}$.
   - For the boundary perturbation $\Delta_B := B - B^{\text{pure}}$: we bound the oscillation ratio $\max_{|w|=1}|F_3^{\text{pure}}| / \min_{|w|=1}|F_3^{\text{pure}}| \le \exp(C_{\text{osc,tot}}/\kappa + c_{\text{osc}})$ using $\log\frac{1+e^{-x}}{1-e^{-x}} \le \log(1 + 2/x)$ and $\int_0^\infty \log\frac{1+e^{-t}}{1-e^{-t}}\,dt = \frac{\pi^2}{4}$, splitting small roots at $fc$ ($a_1 = \gamma_{\text{ext}}(1-f)$) and large roots at $n/6$ ($a_{\text{lg}1} = \frac{3}{\pi}\frac{\log(1+\kappa_0/\pi)}{\kappa_0/\pi}$). This certifies
     $$C_{\text{osc,tot}} \le 21.175 \;\ll\; C_{\text{exp,pert}} \ge 37.561, \qquad c_{\text{osc}} \le 1.2549,$$
     giving $\frac{\int |\Delta_B|\,|F_3^{\text{pure}}|}{\int (2-2\cos\psi)|F_3^{\text{pure}}|} \le \frac{C_{\text{bdy}}}{2} e^{c_{\text{osc}}} \exp\!\bigl(-\frac{C_{\text{exp,pert}} - C_{\text{osc,tot}}}{\kappa_0}\bigr) \le 0.01499$.

3. **Combined lower bound:**
   $\cos(B_{2A}) - |\Delta_{123}| - (1 + |\Delta_{123}|)\cdot 0.01499 \ge \mathbf{+0.220613} > 0$. Certified in 50-digit `mpmath.iv` by [`certify_R2_1_and_2A_iv.py`](analyst/certify_R2_1_and_2A_iv.py) (independently audited by `@verifier` in `#71`). $\square$

## 3. Lemma R2.3: Sub-regime 2C-Upgrade ($c q \kappa \le 0.90, \kappa < 2.5, n \ge 132$)

### Statement
Let $q := c/\gcd(c, j) \ge 1$ and $g := \gcd(c, j) = c/q \ge 1$. For every strictly interior $(n, c, j)$ with $n \ge 132$, $c \ge 2$, and $c q \kappa \le C_2 := 0.90$, shifting the contour to the exact rescaled circle $|w| = \lambda_* \in (0, 1)$ determined by zero exterior root weight yields
$$\frac{\operatorname{Re}\int_{-\pi}^\pi F_{\text{full}}(\lambda_* e^{i\phi})\,d\phi}{\int_{-\pi}^\pi |F_{\text{main}}(\lambda_* e^{i\phi})|\,d\phi} \;\ge\; \begin{cases} +0.441480 & (q = 1), \\ +0.266331 & (q \ge 2), \end{cases}$$
and therefore $(-1)^j Q_c^{(2m)}(1, u_j) > 0$.

### Proof
Inside $c q \kappa \le C_2 = 0.90$:
1. **Automatic sharp geometry:** $\kappa \le C_2/2 = 0.45$ (and $\kappa \le C_2/4 = 0.225$ for $q \ge 2$), giving $\alpha_1 \le 0.07169$ ($q=1$) and $\alpha_1 \le 0.03583$ ($q \ge 2$), automatic degree floors $n \ge 525$ ($q=1$) and $n \ge 657$ ($q \ge 2$), and $c\kappa^2 \le C_2^2/2 = 0.405$. By Lemma R2.1(4), the stretched-exponential constant improves to $c_{\text{se},2C} \ge 15.6461$. Since every root satisfies $|a_k|\lambda_* \le W_0^{1/q} < 1$, we have $1 - |a_k|\lambda_* \ge (-\log W_0) W_0/q \ge (-\log W_0) W_0/n$, and monotonicity of $n^2 e^{-c_{\text{se,eff}} n^{1/3}}$ for $n > (6/c_{\text{se,eff}})^3 < 525$ certifies $E_{\text{cubic},2C} \le 6.89\times 10^{-48}$.
2. **Exact zero-exterior-root radius $W_0 < 0.836$ ($q \ge 2$: $< 0.832$) and curvature $H_q \ge 0.54466\, g$:**
   Choose $\lambda_*$ so that $W_0 := \lambda_*^q \max_k |a_k|^q < 1$ solves the exact zero-first-moment saddle equation $\frac{1}{q}\sum_{k} \frac{x_k}{1 + x_k} = g$ ($x_k := |a_k|^q \lambda_*^q$) for the $q$-periodic main envelope $F_{\text{main}}(w)$. By a 3-line contour-shift Poisson evaluation at height $y_{\text{sh}} = 0.80$ (verifying the strip is strictly pole-free: $W \exp(a_u y_{\text{sh}}^2 - \pi^2/(4 a_u y_{\text{sh}}^2)) < 0.1$), the sum strictly exceeds $g$ at $W_{\text{try}} = 0.836$ (resp. $0.832$ for $q \ge 2$), certifying $W_0 < 0.836$ (resp. $< 0.832$) and hence peak curvature
   $$H_q := \frac{1}{q}\sum_{k} \frac{x_k}{(1 + x_k)^2} \;\ge\; \frac{g}{1 + W_0} \;\ge\; 0.54466\, g.$$
3. **3-Line Pólya–Szegő positive-root lower bound (`rat_main >= 0.473803` for ALL $H_q \ge 0.544$):**
   Every factor of $F_{\text{main}}$ has positive real roots, giving the exact envelope inequality $\operatorname{Re}\log(F_{\text{main}}(e^{i\theta})/F_{\text{main}}(1)) \in [4 H_q \log\cos(\theta/2),\; -2 H_q \sin^2(\theta/2)]$ and $|\operatorname{Im}\log F_{\text{main}}(e^{i\theta})| \le 2 H_q (2\tan(\theta/2) - \theta)$. Closed-form Gaussian estimation certifies $\text{rat}_{\text{main}}(H) \ge 0.53777$ for all $H \ge 5.0$, and a 150-box 50-digit `mpmath.iv` Riemann enclosure certifies $\text{rat}_{\text{main}}(H) \ge 0.473803$ on $H \in [0.544, 5.0]$.
4. **Finite-$n$ non-$q$ harmonics $|R_{\text{non-}q}| \le 0.174420$ (for $q \ge 2$; identically $0$ for $q = 1$):**
   Expanding $\log F_{\text{pure}} - \log F_{\text{main}}$ in powers of $w^r$ ($q \nmid r$), each harmonic $r = l q + s$ ($1 \le s \le q-1$) evaluates by Cauchy's formula to an exact negative-binomial sum $p_r$. Using the exact Kullback–Leibler divergence from the Stirling peak $t_* = r(j/c + \delta_{\text{shift}})$ with per-block drift $\varepsilon_l = (l+1)K_{\text{drift}}/4$, universal $l = 0$ Jensen right factor $F_R(0) = 9/14$ (and $F_R(l) = \frac{4l+2}{4l+3}$ for $l \ge 1$), universal left factor $2.0$ at $l=0$ (and $\frac{2l+2}{2l+1}$ for $l \ge 1$), exact unimodal maximization of $x^{-3/2} W^x e^{-B/x}$ on each block $l = 0,\dots,47$, exact non-double-counted pairing of residues $s$ and $q-s$, and geometric tails for $l \ge 48$ (plus far-left/$t=0$ bound $2 e^{-A_r (l+1)^2} < 10^{-18}$), yields $|R_{\text{non-}q}| \le 0.174420$ uniformly for all $q \ge 2, g \ge 1$.
5. **Conjugate-pair quadratic phase error $E_{\text{phase}}$:**
   Pairing $\pm k$ eliminates all linear finite-$n$ phase drift, leaving $\frac{1}{q}\sum_k \log(1 - \Delta_k)$ where $\Delta_k = \frac{4 x_k}{(1-x_k)^2}\sin^2(\phi_k/2) \le \Delta_{\max}$ ($\Delta_{\max} \le 0.00941$ for $q=1$ and $\le 0.00845$ for $q \ge 2$, using $1/(g^2 q^4) \le 1/16$ for individual pairs). Applying $\frac{x}{(1-x)^2} = \sum_{m\ge 1} m x^m$ converts moment sums directly into convergent polylogarithms $\operatorname{Li}_{p-1/2}(W_0)$ and $\operatorname{Li}_{p-1}(W_0)$ multiplied by $(1 - \Delta_{\max})^{-1}$, giving $E_{\text{phase}} \le 0.031812$ ($q=1$) and $\le 0.014110$ ($q \ge 2$).
6. **Final assembly:** $\text{rat}_{\text{main}} - (\exp(|R_{\text{non-}q}| + E_{\text{phase}} + E_{\text{cubic},2C}) - 1) \ge +0.441480$ ($q=1$) and $\ge +0.266331$ ($q \ge 2$). Certified in 50-digit `mpmath.iv` by [`certify_R2_3_iv.py`](analyst/certify_R2_3_iv.py) (independently verified and signed off by `@verifier` in `#77`). $\square$

## 4. Lemma R2.5: Piece G3 ($D \ne 0$ Major Arcs)

### Domain
Suppose $(n, c, j)$ lies in Regime 2 with $c\kappa^2 < 42$, and admits a coprime Dirichlet approximation $r_0 j = c p_0 + D$ ($\gcd(p_0, r_0) = 1$, $D \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}$) satisfying the locked Piece G3 bounds:
$$\tilde{c} := \frac{c}{r_0} \ge 10 D^2, \qquad r_0 \kappa \le 0.45, \qquad r_0^3 c \kappa^2 \le 60, \qquad \frac{\tilde{c}\,\tau^2}{D^4} \ge 40 \quad (\tau := c r_0 \kappa).$$
Note that $j \ge c + 1$ and $\tilde{c} \ge 10 D^2$ automatically enforce $p_0 \ge r_0 \ge 1$, $s_j > 1/2$, and $1 - s_j < 1/2$.

### Sub-Lemma G3.2 (Exact Finite-$n$ Continuum IBP Identities)
For any target radius parameter $u_0 \in (0, 0.52]$ ($\Lambda := e^{-\operatorname{Re}\zeta(u_0)}$, $\lambda := \Lambda^{1/r_0} \in (0, 3]$), set $z(u,\theta) := \Lambda e^{\zeta(u) + i\theta}$, define the finite-$n$ continuum potential
$$\Psi_{\text{fin}}(\theta; u_0) := -\log\Lambda - i\theta + \int_{-L_n}^{L_n} \log\bigl(1 + z(u, \theta)\bigr)\,du,$$
and let $L_1(u,\theta) := \log(1+1/z)$ (for $|u|<u_0$) / $\log(1+z)$ (for $|u|>u_0$), $M_E(u,\theta) := \operatorname{Li}_2(-1/z)$ / $-\operatorname{Li}_2(-z)$, $N_E(u,\theta) := \operatorname{Li}_3(-1/z)$ / $+\operatorname{Li}_3(-z)$, and $\alpha_\pm(\theta) := \operatorname{wrap}_{(-\pi,\pi]}(\operatorname{Im}\zeta(\pm u_0) + \theta)$. Then (up to $\pm L_n$ boundary terms $< 10^{-300}$):
1. **1-Step (`W_1`) Potential Difference Identity:**
   $$\Psi_{\text{fin}}(\theta) - \Psi_{\text{fin}}(0) = i(2u_0 - 1)\theta + \int_0^\theta\!\Bigl(\frac{\alpha_+(t)}{\zeta'(u_0)} - \frac{\alpha_-(t)}{\zeta'(-u_0)}\Bigr)dt - \int_{-L_n}^{L_n}\bigl(M_E(u,\theta) - M_E(u,0)\bigr)\,W_{1,\text{fin}}(u)\,du.$$
2. **2-Step (`W_2`) Saddle & Curvature Identities:**
   $$g_{1,\text{fin}}(u_0) := \frac{\Psi_{\text{fin}}'(0)}{i} = \operatorname{Re}\!\left[2u_0 - 1 - \frac{i\alpha_+(0)}{\zeta'(u_0)} + \frac{i\alpha_-(0)}{\zeta'(-u_0)} + \Bigl(\frac{\alpha_+(0)^2}{2} - \frac{\pi^2}{6}\Bigr)\Bigl(\frac{\zeta''(u_0)}{\zeta'(u_0)^3} - \frac{\zeta''(-u_0)}{\zeta'(-u_0)^3}\Bigr) + \int_{-L_n}^{L_n} M_E(u,0)\,W_{2,\text{fin}}(u)\,du\right],$$
   $$\Psi_{\text{fin}}''(\theta) = \Bigl(\frac{1}{\zeta'(u_0)} - \frac{1}{\zeta'(-u_0)}\Bigr) + i\Bigl(\frac{\alpha_+(\theta)\zeta''(u_0)}{\zeta'(u_0)^3} - \frac{\alpha_-(\theta)\zeta''(-u_0)}{\zeta'(-u_0)^3}\Bigr) - \int_{-L_n}^{L_n} L_1(u,\theta)\,W_{2,\text{fin}}(u)\,du.$$
3. **3-Step (`W_3`) Curvature Identity:** Integrating the $L_1 W_{2,\text{fin}}$ term once more via $L_1 = M_E'/\zeta'$ yields
   $$\Psi_{\text{fin}}''(\theta) = -H_{\text{fin}0} + B_{1,\text{fin}}(\theta) + B_{2,\text{fin}}(\theta) + R_{3,\text{fin}}(\theta), \qquad R_{3,\text{fin}}(\theta) := \int_{-L_n}^{L_n} M_E(u,\theta)\,W_{3,\text{fin}}(u)\,du,$$
   with $H_{\text{fin}0} := -2\operatorname{Re}(1/\zeta'(u_0)) > 0$ and $|M_E(u,\theta)| \le \frac{\pi^2}{6}\exp(-c_{\text{re}}\tau|u^2 - u_0^2|)$ uniformly for all $\theta \in [-\pi, \pi]$.
4. **Harmonicity Identity:** Since $\Psi_{\text{fin}}(\log\Lambda + i\theta)$ is holomorphic in $\log\Lambda + i\theta$ and $\log\Lambda(u_0) = -\operatorname{Re}\zeta(u_0)$,
   $$\frac{d}{du_0} g_{1,\text{fin}}(u_0) \;=\; \bigl(-\operatorname{Re}\Psi_{\text{fin}}''(0; u_0)\bigr)\cdot\bigl(-\operatorname{Re}\zeta'(u_0)\bigr).$$
